Privacy Policy

Effective: November 24, 2026 · 日本語

This policy explains how "Transparent Mahjong" (the "Game") handles your information. The Game has no account registration and no analytics. The mobile app version shows ads and offers in-app purchases; the Steam version shows no ads.

2. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share your information except as required by law. Services we use: Cloudflare (privacy policy), Valve / Steam (Steam version), RevenueCat (privacy policy) and Google AdMob (ads policy) for the mobile app.

3. Deleting your data

Because the Game has no accounts, no data that identifies you remains on our server. Data on your device (name, settings, records) is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. You can also erase stats and achievements from Settings.

4. Player names

Your player name is visible to the other players at your table. Names containing inappropriate words are replaced automatically. Please do not use your real name or other personal information.

5. Children

The Game does not ask for personal information and involves no real-money gambling. Please use in-app purchases under the supervision of a parent or guardian.

6. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the update on this page.

7. Contact

Please use the support contact listed on the Game's store page (Steam / App Store / Google Play).
